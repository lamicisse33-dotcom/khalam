Meet BIA Xarit, a conversational AI assistant developed in Dakar for spoken Wolof and French.

What is a Wolof AI voice assistant?

A voice assistant combines speech recognition, language understanding and spoken responses. For Wolof speakers, natural pronunciation and everyday expressions matter, especially when a conversation mixes Wolof and French.

Meet BIA Xarit

Developed by KHALAM in Dakar, Senegal, BIA Xarit is designed for spoken Wolof and French. It can help draft messages and quotations, explain photographed documents and organize everyday tasks. Its core conversational languages are Wolof and French; this English guide does not imply that its core voice assistant supports English.

Wolof speech technology

KHALAM is developing and testing KHALAM Voice for speech synthesis and KHALAM Oreille for speech recognition. These research projects are distinct from the currently available application.
